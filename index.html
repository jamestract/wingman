How duos work

A duo is always two people who are in each other's circle, and a friend only counts once you've both confirmed. You never get paired with a stranger, and the feed only ever shows duos.

How matching works

Everyone on both sides privately rates both people across: first choice, open to, or no. A date is only set up if there's a one-to-one pairing where all four of you get someone you rated first choice or open to. Wingman picks the best pairing and tells everyone who's with whom.

Your ratings stay private, even from your friends.
